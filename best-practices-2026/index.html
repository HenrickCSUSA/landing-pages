Compact Inline Flow Sensor

VA 526

Compact. Connected. Future-proof. The newly developed VA 526 combines precise flow measurement, consumption monitoring, temperature and - optionally - pressure and humidity/dew point in one compact inline sensor for compressed air and gases, with WiFi and Bluetooth as standard.

  • Thermal mass flow sensor; air & gases (N2, Ar, CO2, O2, N2O)
  • Accuracy ± 1.5% of m.v. ± 0.3% of f.s. (precision ± 1% on request)
  • Measuring sections 1/4" to 2" (G or NPT); up to 16 bar, optionally 40 bar
  • Modbus-RTU, Ethernet/PoE, IO-Link, WiFi & Bluetooth; 4...20 mA
  • Optional flow straightener - no inlet sections required
  • CS SensorApp for setup & live readings; EU Cyber Resilience Act ready
Online Monitoring

CS TwinAir

A plug-and-play online monitoring device and software for all CS INSTRUMENTS and third-party devices. Connect a Modbus sensor, send data to the cloud, and view current and past values from anywhere - with worldwide GSM LTE & WiFi coverage.

  • Universal compatibility - works with virtually any device
  • Plug & play Modbus M8 connection (1 input for sensor/DATABOX)
  • GSM LTE & WiFi, worldwide coverage (30+ countries)
  • Cloud data logging & visualization with no storage limitations
  • Encrypted server connection; centralized dashboard for all stations
Leak Detection

Leak Detectors

See our acoustic leak detectors live - including the LD 500 / LD 510 UltraCam and the LeakCam 600. Pinpoint leaks, read the leakage rate in cfm, and get the annual cost in dollars right on the display.

  • LD 500 / LD 510: 30 MEMS microphones, on-screen leak visualization
  • LeakCam 600: 64 MEMS mics, Power Beam Forming, detects from up to 390 ft
  • Automatic laser distance measurement for accurate cost calculation
  • ISO 50001 reports via CS Leak Reporter
