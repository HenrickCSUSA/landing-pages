Compressed Air Is Your Most Expensive Utility and the Hardest to See

Compressed air can account for 20–40% of a plant's electricity bill, yet most facilities run it blind. The damage is already done by the time anyone notices.

Silent Leaks Bleed Cash

A typical plant wastes 20-30% of its compressed air to undetected leaks, running 24/7 and draining energy you're paying for whether you produce or not.

Unexpected Downtime

A failing dryer or pressure drop can halt an entire line. Without early warning, you find out when production stops. Not before.

Zero Visibility

Data trapped on the factory floor means no trends, no off-shift insight, and no way to prove savings or justify a fix to management.

Real Financial Impact

Wasted air, energy surcharges, and emergency repairs add up to tens of thousands of dollars a year. That money goes straight to your bottom line once it is controlled.

Did you know?

The VA 526 can measure flow, consumption, temperature, pressure, and dew point - all from a single installation point.

Most inline flow sensors only measure velocity and temperature. The VA 526 goes further with an optional integrated pressure sensor and optional dew point monitoring, giving you a complete picture of your compressed air quality and efficiency without cutting the pipe multiple times.
